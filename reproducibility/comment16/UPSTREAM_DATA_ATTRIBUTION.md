NVBD is an adapted dataset derived from the original
Highway 2 partition records.

The independent replay uses 221 sender/split offsets
recovered from NVBD. It reproduces the published sender
positions but does not recover the original random seed
or the complete 52-column generation process.

The audit and replay scripts are verification utilities,
not the original attack-generation program.
